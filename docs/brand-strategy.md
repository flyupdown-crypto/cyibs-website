# YBS Brand Strategy

## Brand Name
YBS - Cambridge Youth International Business School

## Legal Entity
CAMBRIDGE YOUTH INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS SCHOOL LTD  
Company number: 17210929

## Website Purpose
Build a premium international education brand website for student recruitment, parent trust-building, and consultation conversion.

## Primary Audience
European Chinese entrepreneur families and globally minded young talents.

## Main Programme
Cambridge Summer Business Leadership Programme.

## Secondary Pathway
Long-term academic and business leadership pathway.

## Brand Positioning
A Cambridge-based international business and leadership academy for future global business leaders.

## Visual Style
60% MBA business school  
25% British elite education  
15% Future AI

## Tone
Premium, trustworthy, international, restrained, aspirational.

## Avoid
Chinese training-school style.  
Overly colourful design.  
Cheap animations.  
Unverified accreditation claims.  
Direct implication of affiliation with the University of Cambridge.

## Main CTA
Book Consultation

## Language
English primary.  
Chinese secondary.
